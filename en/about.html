EPISUIS

Applied epidemiology with a systems perspective.

EPISUIS was created for situations where isolated data are no longer enough: health problems involving multiple units, timelines, territories, diagnostic results and decisions.

Purpose Turn dispersed information into better-structured questions, reproducible analyses and useful interpretations for organizations and projects.

Who is behind EPISUIS

Alberto Jorge Galindo Barboza

His work focuses on research and applied epidemiology in swine health, with experience in study design, risk analysis, diagnostics, molecular epidemiology, data visualization, and the design of digital systems for epidemiological surveillance and information management.

Reach

Based in Mexico, with international scope.

EPISUIS is based in Mexico and is designed to collaborate with producer organizations, institutions, production networks, consortia, and national or international projects when the question requires applied epidemiology, information integration, or technical solution development.

Collaboration can be fully remote when data and objectives allow it, or integrated into field projects, working networks, and multidisciplinary teams. Scope is defined before work begins; not every problem requires the same analytical depth.

Separate communication, evidence and consulting.

Technical notes serve as the public archive for explanations and methodological resources. The professional profile documents research, publications and academic work. EPISUIS focuses on commercial relationships and consulting projects.
