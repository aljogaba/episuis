Consulting

The problem defines the method.

EPISUIS does not start from predefined packages. Consulting is designed around the question, system scale, available evidence and the type of decision that needs support.

Approach Applied epidemiology, data integration and solution building. Scope can range from groups of farms to networks, organizations, institutions and regional or collaborative projects.

What a project may integrate

Health, production, diagnostics, territory and time within one question.

Study design + samplingRisk + biosecurityDiagnostics + sequencesSpace, networks + monitoring

Capabilities

Different tools for different questions.

A consulting project may combine several capabilities. They are not separate products; they are resources assembled according to the problem.

Epidemiological design and sampling

Population definition, sampling strategy, follow-up frequency and interpretation criteria.

Risk analysis and biosecurity

Associated factors, exposure patterns, critical points and heterogeneity among units.

Diagnostics and molecular epidemiology

Integration of PCR, serology, sequences, phylogeny and epidemiological context.

Spatial and network analysis

Maps, geographic distribution, links among units, flows and territorial structure.

Data analysis and visualization

Cleaning, structuring, exploratory analysis, graphics, dashboards and interpretation-oriented outputs.

Applied systems and tools

Calculators, dashboards, digital workflows, and capture or monitoring systems developed when a project needs to turn methodology into reproducible operations.

A concrete problem?

You do not need to arrive with the methodology solved.

The first conversation is used to understand the problem, what information exists and what the organization needs to answer.
